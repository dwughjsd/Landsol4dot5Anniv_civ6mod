[h1]爱梅斯、真步（探险家）、镜华（夏日戏水）领导的兰德索尔：先睹为快[/h1]

新的伙伴加入团队——本模组包含了3位《公主连结 Re:Dive》游戏发布4.5周年之际发布的角色，为领导兰德索尔的玩家提供更多不同的统治方式。

[b]爱梅斯[/b]的特色能力是“[b]重始白羽[/b]”。他们开局时获得额外的信仰值。如果高级单位阵亡，他们能以单位拥有的升级为代价，在首都重塑一个。一旦建造圣地，他们可以立即获得大预言家并随后获得一个强力战斗单位“[b]可可萝[/b]”。可可萝有独特的升级树且可以撤退回首都，但在一局游戏中只能获得一次。

公主骑士为爱梅斯提供“[b]繁花祝福[/b]”能力。他们研发新时代的首个科技或市政后能够获得尤里卡和鼓舞。处于黑暗时代时，尤里卡和鼓舞可立即完成科技或市政。爱梅斯可以在建有公会之家的城市开展“入梦”特色项目，完成项目后便可获得专属于他们的记忆碎片巨作，这种巨作在他们手上可提供更多产出。

[b]真步（探险家）[/b]的特色能力是“[b]真步真步毛绒支援[/b]”。侦察单位生产更快，获取经验更多，且在新大陆上获得额外的战斗力。这些加成让他们的探索旅途更快也更安全。特色建筑[b]真步真步营地[/b]让羊、棉花和染料单元格产出额外文化值，在新大陆上还提供忠诚度和金币。

“[b]童话探险[/b]”是公主骑士提供给探险家真步的额外能力。每个来自其他文明的代表团或大使馆都能为他们提供文化值。在一片新大陆上，他们的单位行动更加迅速，建立第一座城市时还可以免费获得开拓者和侦察单位。

[b]镜华（夏日戏水）[/b]的特色能力是“[b]水浪顶尖[/b]”，使他们在海边占据优势。相邻或位于海岸或湖泊的改良设施产出随机数额的额外科技值、生产力或文化值。

与她的领袖能力相配合的是公主骑士提供的额外能力“[b]夏日水弹风暴[/b]”。在沿海地区建设便可为城市带来可观的科技值、生产力和文化值加成。同时，远程单位的攻击可以降低目标单位的战斗力。

您将会如何使用这些新领袖？您将会如何带领兰德索尔建立经得起时间考验的文明？

[h1]我们的风格：[/h1]

[b]有玩法，不超模。[/b]每位领袖的能力组都经过精心设计与测试，使它们能够支持一种独特的玩法。能力强度与官方的强力领袖相当而不会击碎原作平衡。

[b]忠于原作。[/b]我们的作品在制作过程中精细考证了领袖和他们的故事，拥有完整的人名、地名、城市名称和外交用语，以尽最大可能表现原作的风土人情和领袖性格特征。

[b]广泛兼容。[/b]所有已制作的美术素材均参照《文明6》游戏的规格进行处理，确保其在各种分辨率和设置下都能正确显示且整洁清爽。

[b]领袖语音。[/b]使用了原作游戏中的语音片段。不含AI生成内容。

[b]负责任的AI使用。[/b]我们谨慎地使用生成式AI创作的内容，确保不含“数字泔水”。

[h1]已知问题：[/h1]

文明百科仅支持简体中文。

[h1]Ameth, Maho (Explorer) and Kyoka (Summertime) Lead Landosol: First Look[/h1]

New friends join the team - this mod contains 3 characters released near the 4.5 years anniversary of mobile game Princess Connect Re:Dive, providing more different ways to rule for players leading Landosol.

[b]Ameth[/b]'s unique ability is [b]Rifàre Piuma[/b] (literally "Reshap(ing) Feather(s)"). [i](Note: This name might be a mistranslation by the original game of "reshaping wings" since the word for "wing" in Japanese can also be interpreted as "feather", and the Italian words they used seem to be grammatically incorrect. We used the name as is.)[/i] They start with bonus Faith. When a high level unit is killed, they can recreate one in their captial at the cost of its promotions. Once they build a Holy Site, they can gain a Great Prophet at once and later receive a [b]Kokkoro[/b], a powerful combat unit which has a unique promotion tree and can retreat to capital but can only be acquired once per game.

Princess Knight grants Ameth [b]Fiore Felicità[/b] (literally "Flower Happiness"). They earn Eurekas and Inspirations when the first technology or civic of a new Era is researched.  When in a Dark Age, Eurekas and Inspirations can finish research of technologies or civics instantly. Ameth can initiate Enter Dream World unique project in cities with a Guildhouse. By finishing the project, they acquire Memory Pieces, the great works unique to them which provide more yields on their hands.

Unique ability of [b]Maho (Explorer)[/b] is [b]Maho Maho Fluffy Support[/b].  Recon units are trained faster, gain experience faster, and have bonus Combat Strength on a new continent. With this ability, they can explore the world faster and safer. Unique building [b]Maho Maho Camp[/b] provides extra Culture on tiles with Sheep, Cotton and Dyes, and additional Loyalty and Gold when built on new continents.

[b]Fairytale Exploration[/b] is the unique ability that Princess Knight provides for Explorer Maho. Each Delegation or Embassy from a foreign civilization provides extra Culture for them. On a new continent, they can have their units move faster, and receive a free Settler and a recon unit when they found the first city.

[b]Kyoka (Summertime)[/b] has a unique ability named [b]Aqua Crest[/b] which provides advantages when they are near Coasts. Each improvement on or next to Coast or Lake tiles provides random amounts of extra Science, Production or Culture.

The extra ability that Princess Knight provides in synergy with her leader ability is [b]Summer Aqua Flock[/b]. Constructing in coastal areas provides considerable bonus Science, Production and Culture for their cities. In addition, attacks initiated by their ranged units can reduce Combat Strength of the target unit.

How will you use these new leaders? How will you lead Landosol to build a civilization that stands the test of time?

[h1]Our Way of Modding:[/h1]

[b]Yes for Uniqueness, No for Overpowering[/b]: All leaders are designed and tested so that each of their ability sets promotes a unique play-style. They are set to be as powerful as strong official leaders and won't break game balancing.

[b]Lore-Loyalty[/b]: Our mods are made with a fine research of the leaders and their stories, and provides full lists of named places, citizen names, city names and diplomacy statements to better represent lore and leader characteristics.

[b]Optimized for Compatibility[/b]: All art assets included in this mod are processed to fit the need of Civilization VI, so that they can display correctly and neatly on different screens / settings.

[b]Leader Speeches[/b]: Voice clips from the original game are used. Not AI-generated.

[b]Responsible AI Usage[/b]: We use generative-AI-created contents with caution to ensure the mod is slop-free.

[h1]Known issues:[/h1]

Some Civilopedia articles are in Simplified Chinese only.

Names of Ameth's traits are direct restorations of the original Japanese transcriptions of Italian words which might be grammatically incorrect. None of authors of this mod can speak Italian, so we might need some support to have better ability names.
